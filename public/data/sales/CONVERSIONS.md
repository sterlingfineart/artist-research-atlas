# Today's-money conversions — source and method

The Art Sales tab's "Today's-money equivalents" switch converts a
historic sterling price into modern terms with a multiplier looked up
by the year of sale. The multipliers live in `conversions.json` in
this directory and are generated by `scripts/build-sales-data.py`.
No live external service is used.

## Sources

Two published sources are spliced at 2016:

1. **Bank of England, "A millennium of macroeconomic data for the UK"**,
   version 3.1 (compiled by Ryland Thomas and Nicholas Dimsdale),
   sheet **"A1. Headline series"** — used for every year up to and
   including 2016, the last year of that dataset:
   - **By prices** — the dataset's *Consumer price index* (2015 = 100).
   - **By earnings** — the dataset's *Earnings per head* (average weekly
     earnings, £).
   Dataset page: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/research-datasets
   Workbook as used: `a-millennium-of-macroeconomic-data-for-the-uk.xlsx`
   (v3.1), accessed 20 November 2026. A copy is kept with the project's
   source material for reproducibility. Its 2016 values are
   CPI = 100.65975 and earnings = £503.1667 per week.

2. **Office for National Statistics** (ons.gov.uk), accessed
   21 November 2026 — used to extend both series from 2017 to the
   base year:
   - **By prices** — *CPI annual rate* (series **D7G7**, dataset MM23):
     the percentage change in the calendar-year average CPI (all
     items) over the previous year.
   - **By earnings** — *Average Weekly Earnings* (series **KAB9**,
     dataset EMP): whole economy total pay, seasonally adjusted. The
     published annual averages (£ per week) are tabulated below; each
     year's growth factor is that year's average divided by the
     previous year's.

## Method

- **Base year: 2025** — the latest full year for which ONS has
  published complete annual figures. Equivalents are expressed in
  2025 pounds and are labelled as such on the page.
- **Splice point: 2016.** The 2025 index values are the BoE 2016
  values with the nine ONS annual growth rates for 2017–2025
  compounded onto them:
  - CPI(2025) = 100.65975 × 1.376415 = 138.5496
    (cumulative factor from the D7G7 rates below; cross-check: the
    ratio of ONS's published CPI annual-average index values,
    series D7BT, 138.4 / 100.7 = 1.3744 — agreement within the
    rounding of the published figures).
  - Earnings(2025) = 503.1667 × 1.473684 = £741.5088
    (the AWE factors telescope to 728 / 494).
- Multiplier for a sale in year *Y*:
  - by prices: CPI(2025) / CPI(*Y*) = 138.5496 / CPI(*Y*)
  - by earnings: Earnings(2025) / Earnings(*Y*) = 741.5088 / Earnings(*Y*)
  where CPI(*Y*) and Earnings(*Y*) are the BoE values for *Y* ≤ 2016.
- The historic price is first expressed in decimal pounds
  (£ + shillings/20 + pence/240), multiplied, and the result rounded
  to three significant figures and marked "≈". The printed historic
  figure is always the primary number; the equivalent is a reading
  aid, never a replacement.
- Both BoE series are continuous for every year from 1209 to 2016 and
  the splice extends them to 2025, so every sterling record in the
  tab (earliest sale year 1314) has a multiplier.
- Franc-priced records show no equivalent: no French long-run series
  has been adopted yet. Modern landmark sales (1990–2026) are shown
  as reported, without conversion.

## Worked examples (checked by hand)

Multipliers are those stored in `conversions.json` (the BoE index
values for the sale years are as in the workbook: CPI(1911) = 1.201841,
earnings(1911) = 0.983421; CPI(1913) = 1.243674, earnings(1913) =
1.037509; CPI(1823) = 1.200193, earnings(1823) = 0.485791).

- Raeburn, "Mrs. Robertson Williamson", 1911, £33,315:
  - by prices: 138.5496 / 1.201841 = 115.2812 → £33,315 × 115.2812 = £3,840,593 ≈ £3,840,000
  - by earnings: 741.5088 / 0.983421 = 754.0096 → £33,315 × 754.0096 = £25,119,830 ≈ £25,100,000
- Hobbema, "A Woody Landscape", 1913, £15,750:
  - by prices: 138.5496 / 1.243674 = 111.4035 → £15,750 × 111.4035 = £1,754,605 ≈ £1,750,000
  - by earnings: 741.5088 / 1.037509 = 714.7010 → £15,750 × 714.7010 = £11,256,541 ≈ £11,300,000
- Abbott, "Mr. Nollekens with bust of C. J. Fox", 1823, £14 14s 0d (= £14.70):
  - by prices: 138.5496 / 1.200193 = 115.4394 → £14.70 × 115.4394 = £1,696.96 ≈ £1,700
  - by earnings: 741.5088 / 0.485791 = 1526.3942 → £14.70 × 1526.3942 = £22,437.99 ≈ £22,400
